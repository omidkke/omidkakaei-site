A simple framework for a project brief

Clarify the problem, the output, and what done means.

Sample article
This is sample content demonstrating the knowledge base. It is not a published work or professional experience attributed to Omid Kakaei.

What is the problem?

Describe the problem and who would benefit from solving it. Keep the proposed solution separate from the problem itself.

What will be delivered?

Define a concrete deliverable and what is out of scope. A prototype, for example, might demonstrate only one primary journey.

When is it done?

Write acceptance criteria that can be observed. Record who will review the work, its dependencies, and any unanswered questions.
